The Studio

Material intelligence,
shaped by discipline.

Founded in 2010 in Chongqing by an industry veteran with 15+ years of experience, Kun Bloom has grown into a recognized force in architectural quartz. Deep expertise, rigorous R&D, and uncompromising product quality drive every surface we ship.

A professional design and R&D team, patented processes, and real-time quality monitoring ensure every product meets national standards GB 6566-2010 and GB/T 4100-2015. From our partner production bases in Foshan and Quanzhou, we deliver premium wall, floor, and façade quartz tiles to clients worldwide.
